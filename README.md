# Pharma_Sales_Data_Analysis
This project focuses on analyzing global pharmaceutical sales data from 2020 to 2025 to identify sales trends, evaluate product performance, and generate meaningful business insights. 

top 10 Business Qustions and KPIs that Helps to show and resolve the business problem based on past data of the Global Pharma Sales 2020 - 2025

1 : Analyze total pharmaceutical sales.
2 : Identify top-selling medicines.
3 : Find the most profitable pharmaceutical categories.
4 : Analyze monthly and yearly sales trends.
5 : Compare sales across different regions.
6 : Identify Region Wise Total Sales.
7 : Calculate total revenue and profit.
8 : Calculate the percentage of the Medicine by Group of Age Categories.
9 : Create an interactive Power BI dashboard.

Technologies Used: Python, Pandas, PySpark, SQL, Apache Hive, Apache Pig, Power BI, Excel, Matplotlib, and Seaborn.

This project demonstrates an end-to-end pharmaceutical sales analytics workflow by combining traditional data analysis techniques with Big Data technologies and business intelligence tools.

It showcases practical skills in data cleaning, data transformation, exploratory analysis, ETL processing, SQL querying, visualization, dashboard development, and predictive analytics.
